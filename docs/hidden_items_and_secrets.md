# Satisfactory: Hidden AWESOME Shop Items & Easter Eggs

This guide outlines how to unlock the hidden cosmetic items, Easter eggs, and secret collectable cassettes associated with the AWESOME Sink, AWESOME Shop, and exploration.

---

## 🚗 1. The Cyber Wagon (The "Tesla" Driver)

The **Cyber Wagon** is a secret vehicle added to the game as an Easter egg parodying the Tesla Cybertruck. It is intentionally designed to be impractical: it has square wheels, handles extremely poorly, travels at a top speed of only 20 MPH, and contains a single inventory slot.

### How to Unlock & Craft:
1. **Print a FICSIT Coupon**: Generate at least one FICSIT Coupon from your AWESOME Sink.
2. **Sink the Coupon**: Take the printed Coupon and insert it back into the input of the AWESOME Sink (either manually or via a storage container/conveyor belt feeding the sink).
3. **ADA Acknowledgment**: Feeding a coupon back into the sink triggers a special voice line from ADA, acknowledging your "special project."
4. **Purchase the Blueprint**: Open the **AWESOME Shop**. Under the vehicles or special purchase tab, the **Cyber Wagon** blueprint will now be visible. It costs **20 Coupons** to purchase.
5. **Craft the Vehicle**: Once unlocked, you can build the Cyber Wagon using the craft bench or equipment workshop for **10 Reinforced Iron Plates**.

## 🏆 2. The Golden Coffee Cup ("Employee of the Planet" Cup)

The **Golden Coffee Cup** is the ultimate badge of honor in *Satisfactory*, proving your completion of the game's core progression.

### How to Unlock:
* **Complete Phase 4 of Project Assembly**: You must manufacture and deliver all required end-game elevator shipments to complete the final tier of the Space Elevator.
* **Unlock Title**: Upon final shipment delivery, you receive the official title **"Employee of the Planet"**, which automatically unlocks the Golden FICSIT Coffee Cup. It functions like the standard coffee cup but features a premium gold-plated finish.

---

## 🛒 3. The Golden Factory Cart

A gold-coated version of the standard FICSIT Factory Cart.

### How to Unlock:
1. **Obtain the Golden Coffee Cup**: Complete Phase 4 of the Space Elevator to get the cup.
2. **Sink the Golden Cup**: Place your Golden Coffee Cup into the AWESOME Sink.
3. **Purchase the Schematic**: Once the cup is processed, the **Golden Factory Cart** schematic will unlock in the AWESOME Shop. You can then purchase and build it.
   > [!WARNING]
   > In some game versions, deconstructing or picking up the Golden Factory Cart in your inventory may temporarily revert it to a standard Factory Cart. Place it carefully!

## 📻 4. Secret Boombox Cassette Tapes

While the **Boombox** is unlocked under the "FICSIT Specials" tab in the AWESOME Shop for coupons, several cassette tapes must be found in the game world rather than purchased:

| Cassette Tape | Location / Acquisition |
|---|---|
| **The Worst of Goat Simulator** | Free in the AWESOME Shop. |
| **Deep Rock Galactic OST** | Hidden inside a cave near the Jungle Spires/coal nodes. |
| **Songs of Conquest OST** | Found near the Crater Lakes region. |
| **Huntdown OST** | Found near a lake in the western Dune Desert. |
| **FICSMAS Tapes** | Acquired during the seasonal holiday event. |

## 🔒 5. Material-Locked AWESOME Shop Schematics

Many items (especially structural cosmetics, lights, and customizers) are hidden from the AWESOME Shop by default. They do not appear in the shop list until you discover their respective raw material or research them:
* **MAM Research**: Researching paths in the Molecular Analysis Machine (like **Caterium**, **Quartz**, **Sulfur**, or **Alien Organisms**) unlocks associated decorative and functional shop listings.
* **Milestone Progression**: Upgrading Tiers unlocks new category tabs in the shop. If you are missing items, ensure your MAM research trees are fully completed.
